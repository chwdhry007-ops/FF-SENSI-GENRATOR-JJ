Swipe Speed: Ultra Fast

M1887 One-Tap Secret Formula:

Enemy close range me ho toh seedha upar drag karne se goli chest par lock hoti hai. Down-Up J-drag se aim chest friction tod kar seedha head par chadh jata hai.

  1. Crosshair Position: Aim ko enemy ke left ya right side me rakho taaki crosshair WHITE rahe.
  2. Fire Button Action: Button ko pehle halka sa neeche dabao aur fir instantly upar swipe kar do (J-Shape).
  3. Quick Weapon Switch: Jaise hi pehli goli fire ho, turant Weapon Switch dabao taaki recoil na bane.

🔥 The #1 Golden Rule: White Crosshair

Free Fire ka default auto-aim hamesha chest par chipakta hai. Agar crosshair pehle se RED ho aur fir aap drag karoge, toh aim chest se kabhi nahi chhutega!

Pro Secret: Crosshair ko enemy ke pairon ke paas ya thoda door rakhein (aim WHITE hona chahiye). Jab white aim ke sath drag karoge, toh game ka aim-assist bullet ko seedha enemy ke head par snap karta hai.
